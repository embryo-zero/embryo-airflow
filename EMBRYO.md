# Embryo Airflow

Embryo Airflow is Embryo-Zero's fork of [SU2](https://github.com/su2code/SU2) (6c0e5c5, commit 6c0e5c5). It does airflow simulation (wings, nozzles, cars).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (COPYING, LICENSE.md) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
